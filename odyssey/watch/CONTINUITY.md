# The Odyssey, so far: continuity notes

What jars when the 49 performed films (Books 1–24) run back to back in
`films/odyssey/the-odyssey-so-far-part1.mp4` (Books 1–12, 26:56) and
`the-odyssey-so-far-part2.mp4` (Books 13–24, 15:48). Timestamps are in the
assembled part (P1 / P2, m:ss) at the join, i.e. where the dissolve starts.
Built by `tools/watch/assemble.py`; read from the last frame of each film
against the first clean frame of the next, plus a contact sheet of the
assembly every 10 s and frame-by-frame looks at ten joins (review sheets made
in scratch space, not committed).

## The big ones

1. **Odysseus has no consistent face, hair or tunic.** Across the films he is
   several different minifigs:
   - crimson tunic with a grid of round studs, brown bowl hair, black goatee
     (Books 7–9, 22, 23; the "hero");
   - the same build in a *bright magenta-pink* (Books 10, 11, 13, 21-S07;
     Book 12 sits between the two depending on the light). It may be one part
     under different lighting, but on screen it reads as another costume, e.g.
     P1 12:18 (B09-S06 → S08, crimson) against P1 18:20 (B10-S02, magenta);
   - a plain red tunic with no studs (Book 5, P1 6:18–8:07);
   - in Book 6 (P1 8:09–8:52), dressed by Nausicaa's maids, a white robe, a
     blond swept hairpiece and a different, bruised face print; one book later
     (P1 8:54) he is back in brown hair and the studded crimson tunic;
   - in Book 24 his hair reads brown at P2 14:55 and golden-blond at P2 15:05
     (same film, B24-S03), and he stays blond-looking in S05;
   - in Book 22 two faces: a smiling full brown beard (B22-S01, P2 12:05) and
     the black goatee (B22-S02, P2 12:45), a minute apart.
2. **The beggar disguise comes and goes.** P2 0:02–2:00 (Book 13), after Athena
   has disguised him, he is still the hero (studded magenta tunic, brown hair),
   and the last caption of B13-S03 (P2 1:42–2:00) has him say "the way I wear rags" over a
   picture of no rags at all.
   The beggar first appears at P2 2:02 (Book 14) as a white-haired man with a
   *black goatee* and a plain white tunic; from Book 17-S05 to Book 20 (P2
   4:31–9:28) the beggar is a different figure: white hair, *full white beard*,
   red vest with crossed straps. Then at **P2 10:28 (B21-S03 → B21-S07)** he is
   suddenly the hero again, studded magenta tunic and brown hair, while the
   text still has him as the stranger who has not yet revealed himself (the
   reveal is Book 22). That is the most jarring costume change in the film.
3. **Telemachus's hair.** P1 2:03 (B01-S03): curly tan hair and a plain blue
   tunic. From P1 2:49 (Book 2) on: a tall swept-back hairpiece, blue jacket over
   a pink diamond-patterned tunic, kept through Books 3, 15, 20, 22, 24. At P2
   5:46 (B18-S02) a figure in the same blue-and-pink costume wears a brown bowl
   hairpiece instead (seen from behind; it may be another young man, but it
   reads as Telemachus).
4. **Lighting and time of day jump inside books.**
   - P1 5:46 (B04-S04 → S05): a dark brown interior (the wooden horse) cuts to a
     washed-out, almost white beach; and S05 ends on a purple sunset sky.
   - Book 10 alternates: B10-S02 ends at sunset (purple-orange sky, P1 18:55),
     S04 opens in flat daylight, ends at sunset again (Eurylochus, P1 19:47),
     S05 opens in daylight.
   - P1 25:17–25:55 (Book 12): the red-orange Helios sky of S06 dissolves into
     the violet dusk of S07, which ends on a near-black night at Charybdis.
   - P2 13:38–13:40 (B22-S06 → bridge → B23-S04): broad daylight in the hall to
     night, and the night set has nothing outside its walls: the hall floats in
     a black void (also at P2 6:35, the black doorway in B19-S03).
5. **Featureless frames inside B09-S09** (the drinking scene). It opens on
   0.3 s of near-black then flat brown (P1 13:26.0–13:26.3, the camera inside
   a prop), and at P1 14:49.3–14:50.2 a full second of plain beige frame (the
   camera inside a ram or rock) under "Polyphemus gropes at the entrance".
   These are in the source film, not the assembly.

Thinnest: Book 14 (one 25 s scene for the whole of Eumaeus), Books 2, 3, 6,
7, 8, 15, 16, 18 and 23 with a single scene each, and Book 24 with 2 of 9
(no Hades, no peace: the film ends on Laertes restored). Book 9 alone is a
quarter of Part 1. Because of the gaps the story jumps: Book 13 ends with
Odysseus still undisguised; Book 16 is only the reveal and the embrace;
Book 23 is only the bed test (no waking of Penelope, no doubting); Book 21
goes from the suitors failing the bow straight to Odysseus shooting it.

## Sound and picture

- Loudness: the films measured −17.6 to −25.9 LUFS integrated (B01-S02
  loudest, B17-S03 quietest); each is normalised to −19 LUFS (−1.5 dBTP) before
  the joins. See `odyssey/watch/so-far.json` → `loudness_in`.
- No still tails were trimmed: every film already ends 0.15–0.25 s after its
  last caption; the "still" after it is the burned-in caption panel fading
  out, so nothing past a spoken line was there to cut.
- A caption error in the source film B13-S03 (burned in and in its .vtt),
  P2 0:47–0:55: "The shepherd tells Odysseus **you** is in Ithaca and the
  hero suppresses **your** joy" (should be "he is" / "his joy"). The film
  needs re-rendering to fix it; the assembly only copies it.
- The burned-in caption panels and the assembly's `.vtt` say the same thing,
  so with captions switched on in the browser the text shows twice.
