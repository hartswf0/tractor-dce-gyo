The Odyssey in LEGO · motion control

The dope sheet

The film's figures used to hold still between the keyframes while only their faces moved. Each scene now carries a choreography: every figure's servos (root, hips, torso, head, arms, hands, legs) keyed on the voice clock, the way a motion-control programmer drives a practical rig from a timeline. tools/choreograph.js writes the rough pass from the recorded voice, the scene's emotion, the blocking and the action in the text; film-readymades/choreo.js plays it in the take, on twos, as layers over the blocking. Scrub the timeline to see the rendered frame; open a figure to see its channels.
