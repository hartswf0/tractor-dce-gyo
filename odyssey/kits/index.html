The Odyssey Line

Every kit made from the poem so far, in the order the poem tells it. Each is one moment and the one object that performs it, built in real parts at minifigure scale and checked by machine: every part must click to the studs below it, and no two parts may fill the same space. Beside each kit, the frames of the film it came from.
