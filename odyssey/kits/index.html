Flagship · Book XVIII · 7,522 pieces

The Shield of Achilles

In Hephaestus's Olympus foundry, the god hammers five concentric folds of bronze, tin, dark-blue kyanos, and gold into a living cosmos: the revolving Heavens on the central boss, the Cities at Peace and at War, the Thrice-Ploughed Field, the King's Harvest, the Vintage, the Herd of Straight-Horned Cattle attacked by Two Lions, the Sheepfold in the Fair Glen, the Choros of Daedalus, and the mighty stream of River Oceanus.

The object: Two complete architectural systems built from the same procedural LDraw grammar and verified to 0 not clicked and 0 clashes by clicks.py: (1) The 96x96-stud Five-Ring Circular Relief Shield (7,522 pieces) with 10 independent lift-out radial kits (M0-M9) seated on a stepped bronze, kyanos, and gold frame; and (2) Eight Monumental 48x32 Collector's Diorama Stage Sets (17,109 pieces) on Dark Blue (272) and Pearl Gold (297) museum plinths with multi-storey stone townhouses, continuous 54200 barrel-tile roofs, Doric stoas, cyclopean bastions, raised stone tree planters, and brick-built livestock.

every part clicks · no clashes
Large · Book XXIII · 2,232 pieces

The Bed

Penelope has ordered the bed carried out; Odysseus, stung, tells how he built it round a living olive, and she runs to him: the two embrace beside the bed while old Eurycleia stands in the doorway with the lamp.

The object: The olive: its trunk is the bed's near-left post and runs down through the cut-open paving into a mass of roots in the base. The bed's rails and straps are laid through courses of the living trunk, so the bed will not lift out of the room: it is clutched to the ground through the floor. The crown rises between the ceiling beams in leaf. The front wall lifts away.

The workshop

  • The design brief: the rules, the Ideas bar, what the film decided, the whole line, the archaeology
  • The trailers: three options and a teaser with voice and sound (films/trailers/*.mp4), each through three rounds of review
  • The performance engine: the score (meaning above mechanics), the intent compiler, the metrics of motivated state change, three temperatures and an Ashby homeostat (tools/perform)
  • The creature rigs: Polyphemus, the Laestrygonians, Scylla, the rams, the dogs, Argos and the cattle as articulated LDraw rigs, with gaits, a giant's heavy walk, reach and grope, a herd, and their test films (film-readymades/creatures.js)
  • The dope sheet: every figure's servos keyed on the voice clock (tools/choreograph.js), over the rendered scene, with the acting density before and after
  • The Odyssey in Cascade: five live node graphs (FIELD.IO's Cascade) grown from the project's failures, each cooked to print, motion, a web player and, for a gesture, a checked LDraw kit
  • Cineosis: every scene of the cut read by Deleuze's cinematic signs, and the moving images the film still has to master
  • Left on the table: the three film stacks that do not yet talk, and the joins that make them one film
  • The regulars' cut: the film from 164.6 minutes to 83.9, scene by scene, with nothing longer than four minutes between set pieces
  • The locations and their dialogue: every scene, who speaks where, the landscapes, and the gaps
  • The landscape library: trees, roads, walls, water for every set
  • The wardrobe: the cast dressed from the Bronze Age evidence
