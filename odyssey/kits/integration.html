The Odyssey Line · the integration map

Left on the table

Many experiments in these repositories have pushed toward one film: recorded voices, drawn faces, acting, staging, sound, real places, weather and light. Most of what they learned has never reached the film. Three stacks each hold part of it, and none of them talks to the others. This is the map of the joins that make them one film, and where each stands.

Three stacks

this line's film

Film Butter

The finished sets (sets.js, finish.py, the kits' standard), the blocking, 31 scenes of gate-checked keyframes, the light rig. Silent: stills only, never exported as video; its sound field is empty and its heads are static prints.

film-readymades/build_odyssey.py, odyssey-runtime.js, keyframes.cjs, odyssey/keyframes/

The cinerium

Everything a performance needs: halfworld faces on minifig heads, lip sync, a named pose library, the sound engine (beds, ducking, cues), sky and weather (sun position, storm, rain, fog, lightning), real terrain at real places, camera moves, and an mp4 exporter. It has made voiced Odyssey films, but on borrowed sets, not these.

world/face.js, cinerium.js, sound.js, sky.js, geo.js, film.js, tools/export-film.js, films/

the film's source

Halfworld

The clock: 152 studio recordings timed to the segment, the voice cast, 706 spoken lines with who speaks to whom, the key-beat emotions, the cutting grammar, the music and its mix law. The Regulars' Cut is written on it.

The order

  1. One scene through everything, the opening at the gate (OD-B01-S03): voice clock, captions, faces and lip sync, cutting, the bed, exported as an mp4. It proves the joins 1 to 6 work together before they are spread.
  2. The 31 filmed scenes the same way, then the Regulars' Cut as one film: its edit list already speaks the voice manifest's segment numbers.
  3. Named acting in place of raw angles, so direction can be written in words and checked against the halfworld's key beats.
  4. Sky, weather and real places: the storm, the night, the dawns, and each set's horizon from where it was scouted.
  5. The kits and the wardrobe on screen: the sets and costumes this line built, in the film it came from.
