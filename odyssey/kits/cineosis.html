Monotony, and where the time-images belong

Where the palette is monotonous

  • 14.7 minutes (26.9′–41.6′, 15 scenes, Goat Island and the Cyclops Coast to The Road to the Dead) with no time-image as a primary sign: Or Il Or Sy Fe Im Dm Bi Fe Or So Sy Fe Bi Ls. 9.5 minutes of it (Goat Island and the Cyclops Coast to The Laestrygonian Harbor) have no time-image even as a secondary.
  • 9.4 minutes in Act V (Penelope Retrieves the Bow to The Hall Is Cleared): marks, duels and vectors. This one is right: the bow and the slaughter are the film's action climax, and the only time-image the act needs is the bowstring's sonsign.
  • 6 action-images in a row, 5.5′: Goat Island and the Cyclops Coast to The Blinding.
  • 6 action-images in a row, 4.5′: Antinous Falls to The Hall Is Cleared.
  • Movement-images carry 78% of the cut's seconds as primary signs. Six of the 45 signs are never used (Xl, Mw, Fp, Mf, Se, Le); none of them is asked for by a beat.
  • pT by act (the lab's measure: 14 × the time-image share of seconds): I 3.2, II 1.0, III 3.4, IV 4.5, V 0.8, VI 1.8. Act II (Scheria) and Act V (the hall) are the flattest; Act IV (disguise) is the richest.

Where time-images belong

the tales (IX-XII), 26 min · Pf

Keep the Phaeacian hall in the film as the tales' frame: return to the listening hall at the end of each book of tales (after the Cyclops, after Circe, the intermezzo in XI, at the end of XII). Each return is a mise en abyme (Ma); played with the key beat's irony (the teller is the liar), the frame becomes powers of the false (Pf).

From IX-S02 to XII-S07 the film is twenty-six minutes of action-images with no frame; the Apologoi are the one place the poem itself asks whether the story is true.

the Nekyia · Sp

Stage the dead as regions of the past, not as a queue of interviews: long lateral tracks, each group of shades in its era's dress, the mist continuous, no cut back to a present-day establishing shot.

Assigned already; the renderer's default (auto coverage WIDE/MID/CLOSE per segment) would turn it back into a dialogue scene.

the Sirens · Sn

Drop the bed (score) entirely under the song; alternate the song-held wide with the deaf crew's silent point of view (Op).

The only scene in the poem built on the split between opsign and sonsign.

Calypso's isle · Ba

Hold Odysseus on the shore as a body of attitude before any line is spoken; give Ogygia an empty any-space-whatever wide (V-S01) so the island is felt as suspended time.

Ogygia is the film's first image of Odysseus: seven years in one posture.

the scar · Sd

Cut XIX-S04 and XIX-S05 as one circuit: hold the hand on the scar, dissolve (marked) into the hunt, dissolve back to the dropped foot. The dissolve is a new renderer capability (see motion: transitions).

The Auerbach digression is the poem's model recollection-image; the cut already keeps both scenes.

recognitions · Lo

Treat each recognition by its own test: Helen (IV) and Argos (XVII) as icons, Telemachus (XVI) as limpid and opaque (no memory), Eurycleia (XIX) as the index of lack opening a recollection, Penelope (XXIII) and Laertes (XXIV) as strong destiny through a marked flashback.

Recognitions are the film's summit events; a single 'emotional close-up' treatment would flatten six different signs into one icon.

the hall of death · Pp

Render Theoclymenus's vision inside the ordinary feast, not as a cut-away: the blood on the walls and the shades in the doorway in the same wide as the laughing suitors.

The film's only peaks of the present; marking it as a dream (Rd) would lose it.

Helen's two tales · Pf

Play Helen's tale (IV-S03) and Menelaus's (IV-S04) back to back without ranking them: the same Troy set, contradictory Helens.

The Telemachy's one time-image, and it costs nothing: both scenes are kept.

2Sea states: calm, swell, surf, the sea as ground◐ partial 33/4321.18

Excellent. The LEGO Movie's sea is built from parts: rows of 1x1 round plates and tiles in trans-blue and trans-clear stepping on twos, foam of white plates at the break; Nolan's trailer keeps the sea a horizon a third up the frame, black at night with one warm light (frames 47, 51).

We have. Static brick water in the landscape library (landscape.py: clear tiles over blue, foam, deep water, beach with shingle and shallows); the game's studded sea plane with a texture that can scroll; sea and sea-calm synthesised beds.

Missing. No sea that moves in take or trailer mode: water is a still surface; no swell height, no surf line, no wake behind a hull, no difference between calm, swell and storm except the sky.

Technique. How the edit uses it

  1. Cut by the scene's primary sign, not by the book. The book tempo (the syncwatch's RHYTHM) is the default; the score's cut_rhythm overrides it where they disagree. They disagree in 17 scenes, almost always a slow sign in a fast book: the Sirens, the lotus-eaters, Argos, the father revealed. Hold those.
  2. Every secondary sign gets one shot, not a mood. A secondary is a shot you owe the scene: the empty throne (Dm) in the council, the deaf crew's silent view (Op) at the Sirens, Ajax turning away in silence (Sn). If a secondary has no shot, drop it from the score rather than spreading it thin.
  3. Recognitions are cut by their own test. Helen and Argos are icons (hold the face past information). Telemachus's is limpid and opaque (he has no memory: no flashback). Eurycleia's is the index of lack that opens a recollection. Penelope's and Laertes's are strong destiny: a marked flashback that returns into the embrace.
  4. Mark a flashback or do not flash back. Strong destiny needs the dissolve, the warmer grade and the return to the face; a told past with no marker is weak destiny and stays in the present shot.
  5. A series is framed the same way every time. Marks (the loom, the rams, the begging hand, the axes, the scar, the thrown stools) only read if each term has the same lens, the same height and the same length. The demark (the lead ram last, the dogs silent, the beggar stringing the bow) is framed in that same set-up with the aberration in it.
  6. Monsters in faces first. The trailer's rule and the icon's operation agree: the Cyclops, Scylla and the Laestrygonians are shown through the faces that see them; the creature gets the wide and the insert, never the close.
  7. Sound forward means do not cut on words. In a sonsign (the horse, the Sirens, the bowstring, Ajax) the sound is the situation: drop or hold the bed, hold the listener, let the image starve.
  8. Empty frames are shots, not gaps. Any-space-whatever and the opsign need a wide with no figure: Ogygia's grove, the Cimmerian shore, the fogged harbour of Ithaca, the hall full of sulphur smoke. Give them their length.
  9. Do not force variety. Act V is duels, vectors and marks, and should be. Add a time-image only where the score's proposal says the poem supports one.

The direction hook

Each scene in odyssey/cineosis/score.json carries a direction block derived from its signs' operations (the primary at 0.6, the secondaries sharing 0.4). It is not wired into the renderers yet. This is where it would go:

 "shot_bias": {
  "WIDE": 0.4,
  "MID": 0.18,
  "CLOSE": 0.28,
  "OBJ": 0.14
 },
 "hold_min_s": 7.0,
 "move": "hold",
 "empty_frame": false,
 "sound_forward": true,
 "cut_rhythm": "slow",
 "insert_object": "the mast and ropes",
 "subject": "odysseus",
 "book_tempo": "fast",
