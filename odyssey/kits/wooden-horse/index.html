Open the horse

  • Carved, not stackedThe horse is sculpted a plate at a time, three times finer than a brick, so its contours run like the grain of carved wood. Every surface that faces up is finished in tiles: no studs on the skin.
  • Nothing overbuiltOne timber colour for the body. Dark brown only where a horse is dark: the mane, the tail, the hooves. Two black plates for eyes.
  • Every part clicksEach part stands on the studs of the parts below it or hangs from those above. tools/forage/product/clicks.py reads every part's real shape and checks the whole build: nothing floats, nothing is held only at a side.
  • A hold that holdsA flat deck inside the barrel with room for five minifigures standing. Posts carry the roof over their heads.
  • A removable flankThe right side, lintel included, comes away as one panel and clicks back in.
  • A cart that rollsPlank deck, bricks in running bond, and four wheels on pinned plates. It runs on its tyres, the way the Trojans dragged the horse into their city.

In the story

The same horse, part for part, stands in the film's Troy: a city built at its scale, with walls twenty-four courses high and the Scaean gate behind it. A minifigure standing beside it does not reach its knee.
