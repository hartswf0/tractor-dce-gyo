The Odyssey in LEGO · behind the scenes

How the Odyssey learned to act

The first LEGO scene of the Odyssey had voices, faces and a set, and the figures did nothing at all. This page keeps the whole process log: every stage a scene went through, and every take of every scene that was shot again, side by side with what changed and why. Nothing is thrown away; a re-shoot moves the old film to the archive before the new one takes its place.

The making-of film

A LEGO film about making the LEGO film, acted by minifigures in a LEGO film studio and performed by the same engine as the Odyssey's scenes, in two parts. In the first, the director asks, the agent builds, the cinematographer shoots, Odysseus waits and then acts, and the examiner reads the record; every number she says is from the forensic report. The second part, after the writers' room's bible (Hearts of Plastic), plays the origins chapter straight-faced: the halfworld's prompt card and its atlas, the loom of agents building eight books at night in one tree, the session cap that cuts an agent off mid-word, the sweeper whose one broom stroke (git add -A) commits three other books' unrendered scenes, a stage direction read aloud as a line, and the bridge into LEGO, with the mandates arriving as a calm voice from a loudspeaker and a First AD translating the Director's one-word calls. Every gag cites its commit in its scene’s experiment card (S05, S06, S07, S08).

How a re-shoot works now

A re-shoot never overwrites. The new cut is rendered beside the published film as <scene>-performed-r2.mp4. Before it is renamed into place, tools/perform/keeptake.py moves the current film (with its poster, captions and shot data) to films/odyssey/takes/<scene>/take<N> and writes its entry in films/odyssey/takes/index.json, which this page reads. The takes made before that rule were recovered from the repository's history (tools/making/takes_from_git.py), the same stored files, so nothing grew.
