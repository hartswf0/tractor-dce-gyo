The Cinematographer

The performance engine makes the figures act: a score of stimuli, intents, actions and contacts, each with its causes, and a motion, causal and media heat for every figure at every drawing. The shots were still planned from the blocking alone. Now they are chosen from the performance: the camera goes where the heat is, cuts when the heat moves, and every camera is tested against the real set before it is used.

Code: tools/cinematographer/plan.js (the plan, from the score), tools/cinematographer/solve.js (the cameras, in the player), the hook in film-readymades/odyssey-take.js. The engine: the performance engine.
