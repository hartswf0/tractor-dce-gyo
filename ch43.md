# Chapter 43
## When THE SPECTATOR meets THE PARASITE:
## The Midnight Supper

**Michel Serres**

In the House of Orgon, a feast is laid out, but the master is not eating. He is watching.

A figure sits at the head of the table. He is **Tartuffe**. He eats everything. He directs the conversation. He makes love to the master’s wife.

One guest, **THE SPECTATOR** (representing Traditional Criticism/Morality), watches with disgust.

‘He is a hypocrite’, says The Spectator. ‘He is a fake. He is eating the master’s food and stealing his life. He must be expelled. He is the excluded third.’

But another figure watches from the shadows. He is **THE PARASITE** (representing Serres/The Analyst). He sees something else.

‘You are wrong’, says The Parasite. ‘Tartuffe is not a hypocrite; he is an *Analyst*. He enters the Black Box of the family. He intercepts the relations. He creates a crisis. Without him, the family was a "Simplex"—paralyzed, static. He catalyzes the system. He forces it to evolve.’

The Parasite points to the stage.

‘Look at the master’, he says. ‘Orgon loves Tartuffe. He needs him. Why? because the master needs an intermediary. He needs a lens to see his own life. Tartuffe is the included third. He is the observer who changes the system by observing it.’

Suddenly, the scene shifts. We are no longer in Moliere’s play; we are in the court of Amphitryon.

Jupiter descends from the sky. He takes the form of Amphitryon. He sleeps with Alcmene.

‘This is the limit’, says The Parasite. ‘The parasite has become God. He has become the General Equivalent. He can be anything—a bull, a swan, a shower of gold. He occupies all positions. He is the White Domino. He is Power itself.’

The real Amphitryon returns. He is furious. He draws his sword.

‘War!’ cries The Spectator. ‘Conflict! Dialectics!’

‘No’, says The Parasite. ‘There is no war. There is only asymmetry. Jupiter has thunderbolts; Amphitryon has a sword. Mercury has a stick; Sosie has nothing. The logic of battle is a lie. The strong never fight the weak; they crush them. War is a comedy played for the audience.’

He points to **Sosie**, the slave, who is barred from the feast.

‘Sosie is the only one who tells the truth’, says The Parasite. ‘He proposes the logic of the Included Third. "Let us both be Sosie," he says. "Let us share." But the masters say No. They choose the logic of the Excluded Third. They choose decision. They choose death.’

The Parasite turns to The Spectator.

‘We must write the comedy of the Host’, he concludes. ‘The Host is the object. The Earth. The Woman. Alcmene, who receives both the god and the man. She is the *Chora*—the space where everything happens. She is the real foundation. The parasite makes the noise, but the host makes the world.’

# ASSESSMENT 63: CANDIDATE "THE SPECTATOR"
**Theoretical Submission:** Moral Judgment / Dialectical Conflict

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate judges the parasite as an intruder to be expelled. Conflict (War) is the engine of history.
*   **Darkness Prior:** High risk of **Naive Realism**. Believing that "War" is a contest of equals ignores the structural asymmetry of power.
*   **Intended Frame:** Moral/Hegelian.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: The Definition of Hypocrisy
*   **Evidence:** "He is a fake... He must be expelled."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Epistemic Failure**. Serres redefines hypocrisy as "sliding under the crisis" (sub-determination). The Spectator sees a moral failing; Serres sees a logical operator. The hypocrite (like the scientist) keeps the options open.
    *   *Verdict:* **Shadow**.

#### Criterion B: The Illusion of War
*   **Evidence:** "Conflict! Dialectics!"
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Strategic Blindness**. As Serres notes, "No one ever waged war without having been sure of winning it." Dialectics is the logic of appearances. Real power avoids battle; it uses the "police squadron."
    *   *Verdict:* **Void**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE SPECTATOR
**Score: 45/100 (Theatrical)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Fatal Flaw:** The belief in the "Fair Fight." The Spectator confuses the stage with reality.

# ASSESSMENT 64: CANDIDATE "THE PARASITE" (Serres)
**Theoretical Submission:** The Logic of the Included Third / The Joker

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate defines the parasite as the "Third" who is both excluded (scapegoat) and included (catalyst). The Host (Woman/Earth) is the foundation.
*   **Darkness Prior:** Moderate risk of **Universalization**. If God is a parasite, is there any transcendence?
*   **Intended Frame:** Cybernetic/Theological.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: The Catalyst
*   **Evidence:** "He analyzes, catalyzes, paralyzes." "Disorganizes the box."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **High Analytical Utility**. This explains the role of the observer in science. The observer is not neutral; he intercepts information and changes the system. Knowledge is parasitic.
    *   *Strength:* Identifies Tartuffe as the "Experimenter."
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion B: The General Equivalent (Jupiter)
*   **Evidence:** "He can be anything... The White Domino."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Deep Insight**. Power is the ability to occupy any position. Jupiter (Gold/Money) is the ultimate parasite because he has no fixed form. He replaces the local with the global.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion C: The Universal Hostess (Alcmene)
*   **Evidence:** "She receives the universal parasite... She is the Chora."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Structural Integrity**. Serres grounds the system not in the Agent (Male) but in the Receptacle (Female). The Hostess is the condition of possibility for the Parasite.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE PARASITE
**Score: 98/100 (Distinction)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Paradigm Shift:** Serres moves us from **Dialectics** (War) to **Parasitism** (Asymmetry).
*   **Final Verdict:** The Parasite wins because he reveals the secret: **Power is invisible; it is the joker in the deck.**
