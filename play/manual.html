    What the audit checks

    A stud joint is one stud of a piece seated in the piece above it. Tiles have no studs; a slope has studs only on its stud row; a window, a door frame and an arch have studs on top and open bottoms. Side-by-side contact holds nothing and counts for nothing. Every piece must reach the ground through a chain of joints; a piece two studs or larger hanging by one joint is weak.
