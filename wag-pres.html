Words Assemble Geometries

Operative Ekphrasis • Words Become Worlds
WAG (Words Assemble Geometry/Grids/Generations) claims words don't describe worlds, they compile them—turning natural language into 3D models + social grids that democratize design and, via Freirean pedagogy, make tech a tool of liberation.

The Challenge

Can we prompt scenarios and explore alternative possibilities?
Exploring spatial possibilities of AI through inverse storyboarding via multimodal synthetic agency.

Imagine if you could build anything just by talking about it—like telling a story, but the computer actually makes what you're saying appear right in front of you in 3D. You don't need fancy degrees or complicated software. You just say "make a red box" or "build me a little house" and it happens. We call it a "Wild Ass Guess" approach because you try things out, see what works, and learn as you go.
