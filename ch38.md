# Chapter 38
## When THE PRODUCER meets THE PARASITE:
## The Diagonal of Power

**Michel Serres**

On a dusty road, a giant crawls on his hands and knees. He is **THE PRODUCER** (representing Matter/Energy/Infrastructure). He has immense strength but cannot walk. He is Tantalus, dying of hunger beside a banquet he cannot reach.

He sees a figure stumbling blindly nearby. This is **THE PARASITE** (representing Information/Voice/Superstructure). He has eyes but no strength. He has a voice but no food.

‘Hey!’ calls The Producer. ‘You are going to fall into a well. Come here. I will carry you. You will be my eyes. I will be your legs. It is a fair exchange. Solid for Solid.’

The Parasite climbs onto The Producer’s shoulders.

‘Go left’, whispers The Parasite. ‘Watch out for that stone.’

They move forward. The Producer sweats; The Parasite speaks. The Producer burns calories; The Parasite burns information.

‘This is a new logic’, thinks The Parasite. ‘I am not trading matter for matter. I am trading *Voice* for *Force*. I am trading Air for Roast. I am crossing the exchange. I am making a diagonal.’

‘Where are we going?’ asks The Producer, struggling under the weight.

‘To the palace’, says The Parasite. ‘Trust me. I see the path. You are blind.’

But The Parasite realizes something. If The Producer could see, he would not need a rider. He would throw The Parasite off.

‘Close your eyes’, The Parasite commands. ‘The light is dangerous. Only I can interpret the signals. I will edit the world for you.’

The Producer obeys. He becomes a machine of pure energy, guided by a signal of pure information. He becomes a horse with blinders. A ship with a helmsman who has no porthole.

‘I am the Governor’, The Parasite declares. ‘I am the Cybernetician. I do not touch the world; I touch the relation. I play the position, not the content. The one who plays the position always beats the one who plays the content.’

He looks down at the sweating giant.

‘You think you are the master because you are strong’, says The Parasite. ‘But power is not muscles. Power is the theft of information. I withdraw knowledge from you, and I give you orders. I am the noise that became the signal. I am the joke that became the law.’

The Producer trudges on, carrying his master, believing he is part of a symbiosis, unaware he is part of a bureaucracy.

# ASSESSMENT 53: CANDIDATE "THE PRODUCER"
**Theoretical Submission:** Materialism / Infrastructure / Labor

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate represents the physical base of society—labor, energy, matter. The logic is exchange of equivalents (Solid for Solid).
*   **Darkness Prior:** High risk of **Blindness**. By focusing on the object ("The Content"), the Producer ignores the network ("The Relation") and is easily exploited.
*   **Intended Frame:** Marxist/Industrial.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: The Logic of Substance
*   **Evidence:** "Solid for solid... Meal for coin."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Obsolescence**. The Producer believes in fair exchange. He fails to see the "Diagonal" where Information trades for Energy. He is stuck in the 19th century.
    *   *Verdict:* **Shadow**.

#### Criterion B: The Paralysis
*   **Evidence:** "Crawling on hands and knees... Dying of hunger."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Dependency**. Energy without direction is useless (entropy). The Producer needs the Parasite (Information) to navigate, but this need becomes his enslavement.
    *   *Verdict:* **Void**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE PRODUCER
**Score: 50/100 (Powerful but Blind)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Fatal Flaw:** The Naivety of Content. The Producer plays the game of things (making, moving); he does not play the game of relations.

# ASSESSMENT 54: CANDIDATE "THE PARASITE" (Serres)
**Theoretical Submission:** Cybernetics / Bureaucracy / Information Theory

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate defines power as the control of information ("Cybernetics" = Steersman). The Parasite exchanges "Voice" (soft power) for "Roast" (hard power).
*   **Darkness Prior:** Moderate risk of **Cynicism**. Is all government just parasitism? (Serres argues yes, structurally).
*   **Intended Frame:** Cybernetic/Political.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: The Cybernetic Exchange
*   **Evidence:** "He offers words for force... Air for solid."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **High Analytical Utility**. This explains the transition from Industrial to Information Society. The Manager (Parasite) rides the Worker (Producer).
    *   *Strength:* Identifies "The Diagonal" (Info $\leftrightarrow$ Energy) as the new axis of power.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion B: The Blinding Strategy
*   **Evidence:** "Eyes of the producers have to be poked out... Bureaucratic power."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Deep Insight**. Power maintains itself by creating artificial rarity of information. The helmsman sees; the oarsmen row blindly. This is the definition of Bureaucracy.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion C: Position vs. Content
*   **Evidence:** "The one who plays the position always beats the one who plays the contents."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Strategic Dominance**. The Parasite operates on the *relation*, not the *thing*. He is the switch, the sorter, the middleman. This is the logic of platforms, banks, and algorithms.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE PARASITE
**Score: 99/100 (Distinction)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Paradigm Shift:** Serres redefines Cybernetics not as "control" but as "parasitism."
*   **Political Theory:** Power is not violence (too simple) or law (too just); it is the **interception of messages**.
*   **Final Verdict:** The Parasite wins because he occupies the crossroads. **To govern is to be carried.**
