Vader's TIE · twelve toys

Stepping stones

Each one isolates a single behaviour, over the ground at Hlíðarendi. None of them is the control scheme. Fly them on the phone.
  1. 01
    The face is freeA TIE moving perfectly straight while you rotate it freely through 360°.
  2. 02
    The lureA stationary TIE attached to a draggable camera by an elastic spring.
  3. 03
    Throw itA TIE you grab directly with one finger, pull backward, and throw.
  4. 04
    One wingA ship missing one LEGO wing whose asymmetry genuinely changes its movement.
  5. 05
    FlickA craft controlled only through flick impulses, with no joystick.
  6. 06
    The camera will not turnA camera that refuses to rotate when the spacecraft rotates.
  7. 07
    FuturesThree translucent projected futures around a continuously moving ship.
  8. 08
    The towerA single skyscraper whose proximity bends the TIE's trajectory.
  9. 09
    CrossfireA target that can only be hit by pointing one way while travelling another.
  10. 10
    RunawayA ship that gets farther from the camera under acceleration until the camera must chase it.
  11. 11
    Shift a pieceA LEGO piece you move from one side of the craft to the other during flight, changing how it turns.
  12. 12
    ApartA TIE that never automatically aligns nose and velocity at all.
