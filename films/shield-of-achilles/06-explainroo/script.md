# How the Shield Was Built: Zero Loose, Zero Clashes

> Architectural explainer for `shield_diorama_lib.py` and `kitlib.check()`.

## hook
In October twenty twenty-six, [#failed] our first Shield of Achilles render failed with [#clashes] ninety-four collisions and floating bricks. [#rebuilt] Here is how we rebuilt all twenty kits with zero loose parts.

## plinth
First, [#cross] every section starts with two cross-laminated plate layers. [#border] On the forty-eight by thirty-two dioramas, six hundred and forty dark-blue and pearl-gold plates [#lock] lock the stage into one rigid slab.

## primitives
Second, [#lib] shield diorama lib replaces flat checkerboards with [#hills] stepped SNOT relief hills, [#masonry] coursed stone walls with jumper cornices, [#trees] stud-bonded cypress trees, and [#beasts] articulated oxen and lions.

## checker
Finally, [#bfs] kitlib check runs a breadth-first stud graph search [#aabb] and a three-D bounding-box clash test across all [#total] twenty-four thousand six hundred and thirty-one pieces before a single frame is rendered.
