Ball Jar – Courage · MENTO 373

Ball Jar is a compact studio shell that pairs the Courage viewer with the MENTO 373 control surface. Instead of hosting its own GLB scene, MENTO 373 reads Courage's live Three.js scene via MomentoInterface and _captureData, mirroring the stage render back into its own viewport and driving Courage's camera and lighting.

Use Ball Jar when you want a focused environment where a single WebGL stage (Courage) is controlled by a film-studio brain (MENTO 373) without the overhead of the full Shield-of-Achilles shell.
