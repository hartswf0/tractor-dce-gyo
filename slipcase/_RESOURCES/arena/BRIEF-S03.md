# S03 — BUILDERS-GAME   (read slipcase/_PROMPTS/S03__BUILDERS-GAME.txt first)
"'Slab!' is not a prompt until the world knows what bringing a slab means."

You build by CALLS, not coordinates: `bring`, `put ON <port> FACING <d>` / `put HERE FACING <d>`,
`again AT <port>`, `name <noun>`, `instance <noun> AT <port> [MIRRORED]`, roles HERE, LAST-TOP,
BESIDE:<noun>. Build ONE tower with calls, then `name tower` and `instance tower AT p…` for the
other three. Build one wall bay, name it, instance it along the walls. Coin nouns for merlons,
windows, the gate. Named things become submodels in the emitted file (that is what the ANATOMY
and REUSE axes see). Record every noun you coin and how many times you instanced it.
Start: `node world.js S03 init`, `tray`, `field`.
Hypothesis: coined nouns reach the reuse/anatomy bands faster and your calls get shorter as you go.
