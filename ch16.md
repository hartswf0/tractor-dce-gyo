# Chapter 16
## When THE ARCHIVIST meets THE WIZARD:
## The Spell of Knowledge

**Terry Winograd**

In the vast Library of Mind, where the books of the world are kept, two scholars are arguing about how to organize the shelves.

One is **THE ARCHIVIST** (representing Predicate Calculus/Uniform Proof Procedures). He is a creature of immense patience and zero imagination. He insists that every fact must be written on a standard index card, and every question must be answered by blindly shuffling the entire deck until a logical match is found.

‘Knowledge’, The Archivist intones, ‘is a set of declarative axioms. We write: *All humans are fallible.* We write: *Turing is a human.* When you ask, *Is Turing fallible?*, I simply run the Universal Proof Algorithm. I try every combination. I try "Turing is a banana." I try "Socrates is a toaster." Eventually, by the laws of logic, I will find the proof. It is pure. It is complete. It is totally independent of the subject matter.’

The other figure is **THE WIZARD** (representing PLANNER/Procedural Knowledge). She wears a robe stitched with algorithms and carries a wand that executes code. She views knowledge not as a static pile of cards, but as a collection of spells—active procedures that know *how* to do things.

‘You are a fool, Archivist’, says The Wizard. ‘If I ask you to prove a thesis is acceptable, you will try to prove every theorem in the library. You have no strategy. You have no *sense*.’

The Wizard waves her wand. ‘My knowledge is Imperative. I do not just say "A thesis is long." I say: *To prove a thesis is long, first check the table of contents. If that fails, count the pages.* My theorems are programs. They have advice. They have filters. They say: *Try this first. If it fails, back up and try that.*’

She points to a robot arm moving blocks in the distance.

‘Look at the Block World. If I tell the robot to "Pick up the block," I am not stating a logical truth. I am giving a command. The robot must *know* that to pick up the block, it must first clear the top. To clear the top, it must get rid of the pyramid. To get rid of the pyramid, it must find a space on the table. This is a chain of procedures, not a syllogism.’

The Archivist shudders. ‘But you are cheating! You are giving the system hints. You are sacrificing Completeness for Efficiency. If a proof exists but your heuristic misses it, you fail. I never fail. I only take... infinite time.’

‘Time is what we do not have’, retorts The Wizard. ‘Real intelligence is not about exploring every path; it is about knowing which path to ignore. My system (PLANNER) allows the knowledge itself to guide the deduction. I can assert: *If you move a block, erase its old position.* I can model the changing world not as a new set of axioms, but as a state-machine that updates itself.’

The Wizard creates a glowing orb labeled "SHRDLU."

‘This is the future’, she says. ‘Not a uniform procedure groping in the dark, but a hierarchical control structure where Syntax calls Semantics, Semantics calls Deduction, and Deduction calls the Robot. Knowledge is not just *what* is true. It is *how* to find the truth.’

# ASSESSMENT 11: CANDIDATE "THE ARCHIVIST"
**Theoretical Submission:** First-Order Predicate Calculus & Resolution Theorem Proving (QA3, Green)

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate represents knowledge as static logical formulas and uses a "Uniform Proof Procedure" (like Resolution) to derive answers.
*   **Darkness Prior:** High risk of **Combinatorial Explosion**. Without domain-specific heuristics, the search space for proofs grows exponentially.
*   **Intended Frame:** Mathematical/Logical.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: Completeness vs. Efficiency
*   **Evidence:** "It guarantees that if any proof is possible... the procedure will eventually find it."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **The Infinity Trap**. Completeness is a theoretical virtue but a practical vice. As the text notes, "Theoretical beauty is not worth the price of low practicality." A system that takes 10,000 years to decide if it can pick up a block is useless.
    *   *Verdict:* **Shadow**.

#### Criterion B: Modeling Change (The Frame Problem)
*   **Evidence:** Requires explicit "State" variables in every axiom (e.g., `ON(A, B, S0)`).
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Bureaucratic Nightmare**. To move one block, the system must prove that every *other* block didn't move. This is the classic "Frame Problem." The Archivist drowns in the bookkeeping of non-change.
    *   *Verdict:* **Void**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE ARCHIVIST
**Score: 45/100 (Fail)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Fatal Flaw:** The refusal to use "heuristic knowledge." By insisting on a "subject-independent" prover, the Archivist throws away the most valuable information humans possess: *strategy*.

# ASSESSMENT 12: CANDIDATE "THE WIZARD" (PLANNER)
**Theoretical Submission:** Procedural Knowledge Representation (Hewitt/Winograd)

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate represents knowledge as executable procedures (`THGOAL`, `THCONSE`). Theorems act as "Demons" that watch for specific goals or assertions.
*   **Darkness Prior:** Moderate risk of **Opacity**. Procedural knowledge is harder to inspect and verify than declarative logic. "Spaghetti code" risk is high.
*   **Intended Frame:** Computational/Heuristic.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: Control Structure (Hierarchical)
*   **Evidence:** "The theorem is a program... THUSE gives advice on what other theorems to use."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **High Efficacy**. This allows the programmer to embed "intelligence" directly into the knowledge base. The system doesn't just know *that* a thesis is long; it knows *to check the table of contents first*.
    *   *Strength:* It solves the efficiency problem by guiding the search.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion B: Handling State Change (The Robot)
*   **Evidence:** Use of `THERASE` and `THASSERT`. "The changing state of the world can be mirrored in the changing state of the data base."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Pragmatic Success**. By treating the database as a "current model" rather than a "set of eternal truths," PLANNER sidesteps the Frame Problem. It simply updates the record.
    *   *Risk:* This sacrifices logical purity. If a theorem is buggy, the database can become inconsistent. But for a robot, survival > consistency.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion C: Integration with Language
*   **Evidence:** Theorems can be direct translations of English commands ("Pick up X" $\to$ `#PICKUP X`).
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Seamless Interface**. The procedural model bridges the gap between *saying* and *doing*. A command is just a procedure call.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE WIZARD
**Score: 92/100 (Distinction)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Paradigm Shift:** The move from "Knowledge as Data" to "Knowledge as Code" is the definitive breakthrough of 1970s AI.
*   **Antifragility:** The system handles failure robustly. If `TC-GRASP` fails to find a clear top, it automatically calls `TC-CLEARTOP` to fix the problem, then retries. This "backup" mechanism is essential for real-world interaction.
*   **Philosophy:** It accepts that intelligence is "circular"—definitions depend on other definitions, and understanding depends on the entire model, not just isolated axioms.

### FINAL COMPARATIVE VERDICT

| Feature | THE ARCHIVIST (Logic) | THE WIZARD (PLANNER) | Guardian Preference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Knowledge Form** | Declarative Formulas | Imperative Procedures | **Wizard** (Actionable). |
| **Search Strategy** | Uniform / Blind | Heuristic / Guided | **Wizard** (Efficient). |
| **State Change** | Explicit State Variables | Database Update | **Wizard** (Simpler). |
| **Goal** | Completeness | Success | **Wizard**. |

**Winner:** **THE WIZARD**.
The Wizard (PLANNER) acknowledges that intelligence requires *know-how*, not just *know-that*. By embedding strategy into the representation of knowledge, it allows SHRDLU to function in a complex (albeit micro) world where the Archivist would be frozen in eternal calculation. **Procedural Embedding** is the key to making the Block World live.
