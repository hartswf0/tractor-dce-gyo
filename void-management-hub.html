The Unified Theory

Void Management is not a single technique but a paradigm shift: construction proceeds not by accumulating parts, but by tracking, constraining, and minimally filling empty space.

The Void

Empty space is not absence—it is potential shaped by constraints. Every part addition is a cost.

The Constraint

Filled cells propagate constraints to neighbors. The lattice evolves with each placement.

The Utterance

Parts are frozen verbs. Assembly is speaking in the grammar of making.

The Disobedience

Strip the script. A croissant is a ribbed semi-torus. A hand is a variable-angle clip.

The Protocol

VOID-FIRST PROTOCOL

1. PARSE narrative → void constraints on 9×9 grid
   Zone 1 (Center): Axis Mundi — what the scene is ABOUT
   Zone 2 (Inner):  Protagonists — active forces
   Zone 3 (Outer):  Environment — context
   Zone 4 (Rim):    Boundary — implied beyond

2. LOOP (max 20 rounds):
   a. ANALYZE: Which zone needs definition?
   b. REQUEST: Bucket of 24 parts from Taxonomizer
   c. SCORE:   Micro-PLoT → (G×0.3) + (M×0.4) + (W×0.3)
   d. CLAIM:   Parts scoring > 0.6
   e. PLACE:   Arrange as vignette
   f. PROPAGATE: Update adjacent void constraints
   g. CHECK:   If fidelity > 80%, propose release

3. GENERATE → Compile to LDraw MPD
