The films

Films shot in the LEGO world: the trailer, the walker assault on Hoth, the speeder chase on Endor, and the cinerium films, the Simpsons intro and two scenes of the Odyssey, where the figures act. Each one is a MENTO program: the same words play it live, wherever you stand, with its score, its foley and its lines.

Six scenes are made to rehearse: each stands on a real LEGO set from the Odyssey forage, has marks on the floor and beats with their reasons, and a part you can take: Rehearse runs a shot again from its marks until you stop it, and your own figure walks the part while the other figures play theirs. The same scenes go to the hand table: HAND BUTTER builds with your hands in front of a camera, and each scene's plan opens there as marks and figures, one stud to the metre. Shoot your own on word to momento: say what to shoot, press Play, press Rec. The world itself is word to world.
