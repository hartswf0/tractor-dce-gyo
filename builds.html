The bar — real LEGO sets

Sixteen actual LEGO sets authored in LDraw, from the Official Model Repository by way of the three.js LDrawLoader example (CC BY 2.0). Every render below was made by loading the set's own .mpd into the same viewer our builders use. The numbers are measured, not quoted: build-kit-index.js resolves each set's submodel tree with matrix composition and reports 80 metrics per kit.

What we built

These are not curated. Each one is whatever the builder produced on a single unattended run at the piece count named — no reruns, no picking the good seed. Where a build looks thin or scattered, that is the state of the generator, and the gauntlet's job is to say so in numbers.

Renders are the LDraw parts library drawn by beta-prime-engine.js at 1400px, flat shaded with edges, quantised to 192 colours. Every .mpd opens in LDView, LeoCAD, Bricklink Studio or any LDraw tool.
