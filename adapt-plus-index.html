42 Debates on Mind, Meaning, and Machine
W.F. Harts · 2024 · Dramatic Dialogues with Guardian Evaluation
Abstract

This archive stages 42 dramatic dialogues between archetypal figures representing competing theories of meaning, knowledge, and intelligence. Drawing from Wittgenstein, Geertz, Winograd, Sontag, Bateson, and others, each chapter dramatizes a foundational conflict in philosophy of language, AI epistemology, and anthropology.

The format is agonistic—staged intellectual combat. THE BISHOP argues for referential meaning; THE FOREMAN counters with language-games. THE ANATOMIST seeks universal human nature; THE ARCHITECT insists on cultural particularity. Each debate is then subjected to Guardian evaluation: forensic audit, dark matter scanning, and adversarial critique.

The result is not a textbook but a tribunal of ideas. Theories are tested under pressure. Hidden assumptions are exposed. Only the robust survive.

The Guardian Method

Each theory is evaluated against a "Darkness Prior"—a systematic skepticism toward hidden assumptions. The Guardian issues three possible verdicts:

◯
Light
Sound argument, robust under scrutiny
◐
Twilight
Mixed—strong core, unexamined edges
●
Shadow
Fatal flaw, hidden assumption exposed
