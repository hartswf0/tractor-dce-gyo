GUARDIAN ASSESSMENT

Fatal Flaw: The "Onion" Fallacy. The Anatomist assumes that if you strip away the particular, you find the universal. Geertz proves that if you strip away the particular, you find nothing functioning at all.

Epistemic Risk: This view blinds science to the reality that humanity is defined by its diversity, not its uniformity.

Paradigm Shift: The Architect successfully replaces the metaphor of "clothing" (hiding the truth) with the metaphor of "software" (enabling the function).
