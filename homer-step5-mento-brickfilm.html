Treat Mento as a Camera Pointed at Courage
Goal of this step: record motion clips from the same Courage stage you've been editing and tuning.

• In HOMER SINGLE mode, clicking MENTO docks Courage + Mento together.
• Courage stays the source of truth for MPD, GOLD, IMG and GLB. Mento is just a camera panel attached to that canvas.
Typical shot flow:
1. Set Aspect = 4:3 and Quality = 960p in Mento.
2. Pick a path: manual orbit (you drive), orbit ring, or push-through-center.
3. Hit REC – the header flips to REC, subtle sound / vibrate confirms capture.
4. Let the move play, then hit STOP.
5. Hit SAVE – downloads mento_<scene>_<timestamp>.webm and pins it to the shot strip.
