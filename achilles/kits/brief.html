The Five Rules of the Shield of Achilles Line

Built on the exact architecture of The Odyssey Line (tools/forage/product/) and grounded in Jay David Bolter & Watson Hartsoe’s Operative Ekphrasis: From Imagetext to Worldtext.

1. The Moment, Then the Room

Each kit stages the exact dramatic turning point in Homer’s Iliad XVIII.468–608: the Revolving Bear on Hephaestus’s anvil, the Two Talents of Gold in the sacred circle, the 1.6× Golden Gods leading the sortie to the river ambush, the golden fallow turning black behind the plough, the nine dogs recoiling from the two lions, the potter’s-wheel Choros inside River Oceanus, and the 80×80-stud UCS Grand Rotunda uniting all ten worlds around the Sunken Fountain Atrium.

2. One Working LEGO Mechanism (“The Object”)

Every kit builds its Homeric motion around a physical LEGO mechanism or staged modular architecture verified in LDraw.

3. The Bronze Age & Panopticon Architecture

Walls are cross-bonded masonry courses and Pylos/Mycenae fresco dados with seeded Mediterranean oaks, olives, cypresses, reeds, and silver-poled vines (landscape.py).

4. Finished Surfaces

Every kit stands inside a tiled architectural border with SDF/voxel courses capped with smooth tiles, leaving studs open only where figures, animals, and trees stand.

5. Proven by clicks.py & Photographed by shoot.py

Every brick and plate is verified against ./ldraw/parts/*.dat bounding boxes via ldbox.py and clicks.py (0 loose · 0 clashes across all 16 builds) and studio-rendered via shoot.py.
