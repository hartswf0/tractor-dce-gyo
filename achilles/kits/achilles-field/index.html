The Dramatic Moment (Iliad XVIII.541–572)

Three georgic worlds divided by dry-stone walls: the thrice-ploughed fallow with a roofed headland wine-pergola and an 8×8 revolving ox-plough cassette turning golden glebe into black furrow; the deep corn where the Silent King rejoices on his plinth before the spreading Oak and ox-roast hearth; and the golden vineyard on silver poles enclosed by a dark-blue kyanos trench, tin fence, and single stone causeway.

The Working Mechanism (“The Object”)

The 8×8 Marvel of the Turning Furrow (61485c01 Turntable) — rotate the yoked ox-team and ploughman cassette so the golden soil physically flips to freshly ploughed black earth.
