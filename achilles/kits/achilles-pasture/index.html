The Dramatic Moment (Iliad XVIII.573–589)

From a roofed timber-and-stone cattle byre (kopros), five sculpted gold-and-tin lofty-horned oxen rush down to a reed-fringed river where two maned, fanged lions tear the foremost bull on an 8×8 ford turntable; four golden herdsmen urge on nine sculpted hounds below a wooded mountain glen with a pitched-roof shepherd’s cottage and dry-stone sheepfold.
