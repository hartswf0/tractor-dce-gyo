# Chapter 23
## When THE PLAIN MAN meets THE ANTHROPOLOGIST:
## The Wisdom of the Ant-Heap

**Clifford Geertz**

In a dusty village square, somewhere between the intricate alleys of the Old City and the gleaming grids of the Suburbs, two figures sit on a wooden bench.

One is **THE PLAIN MAN** (representing Common Sense/Vernacular Wisdom). He wears a faded hat and carries a pocketknife. He believes that rain wets, fire burns, and a man who walks into a stump is a fool. To him, the world is flat, obvious, and requires no explanation.

‘Look’, says The Plain Man, pointing to a dark cloud. ‘It’s going to rain. Better come inside. That’s just common sense. Anyone with eyes can see it. Reality is right there in front of you. Why complicate it?’

The other figure is **THE ANTHROPOLOGIST** (representing Cultural Interpretivism/Geertz). He wears spectacles and carries a notebook filled with scribbles about Zande witches and Navaho hermaphrodites. He believes that "Common Sense" is not a raw truth, but a cultural story we tell ourselves to keep the chaos at bay.

‘You think you are seeing reality neat’, says The Anthropologist. ‘But you are wearing glasses you cannot feel. You say, "Rain wets, come inside." But the Zande boy who stubs his toe says, "Witchcraft!" Is he blind? No. He sees the stump. But his common sense tells him that *stumps don't hurt people unless there is malice involved*.’

The Plain Man scoffs. ‘Witchcraft is nonsense. It’s superstition. Common sense is what’s left when you strip away the nonsense.’

‘Is it?’ asks The Anthropologist. ‘Consider the intersexual—the person born with ambiguous gender. To you Americans, this is a horror. A mistake. You try to fix it with surgery. That is your "Common Sense." But to the Navaho, that same person is a blessing—a leader, a source of wealth. To the Pokot, that person is a "botched pot," useless but not horrifying. Three different realities. Three different "Common Senses."’

The Plain Man shifts uncomfortably. ‘Well, maybe about weird things. But practical things? Knowing a hawk from a handsaw?’

‘Even there’, says The Anthropologist. ‘Common sense claims to be practical, natural, thin, and accessible. It says: *Truth is plain as a pikestaff.* But this "thinness" is a construction. The Javanese woman who says a spirit pushed her grandson out of a tree is being literal. She isn't using a metaphor. To her, the spirit is as real as the tree.’

The Anthropologist opens his notebook. ‘Common Sense is a cultural system. It is a set of "gnomic utterances"—proverbs, jokes, warnings—that claim to be wisdom. "Look before you leap," but "He who hesitates is lost." It is immethodical. It is a "potpourri of disparate notions." It is not science. It is not logic. It is the wisdom of the ant-heap.’

The Plain Man looks at the cloud again. It has started to sprinkle.

‘You talk a lot’, says The Plain Man, opening his umbrella. ‘But you’re still getting wet. That’s a fact.’

‘It is a fact’, agrees The Anthropologist, opening his own umbrella. ‘But the *meaning* of the wetness—whether it is a nuisance, a blessing, or a failure of witchcraft—that is where the culture lives. We must stop treating Common Sense as the background. We must treat it as the subject.’

# ASSESSMENT 23: CANDIDATE "THE PLAIN MAN"
**Theoretical Submission:** Naive Realism / Universal Common Sense

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate asserts that Common Sense is a direct apprehension of reality ("The world is its authority"). It is universal, practical, and unconstructed.
*   **Darkness Prior:** High risk of **Ethnocentrism**. Assuming one's own cultural assumptions are "just natural" is the classic error of the unexamined mind.
*   **Intended Frame:** Phenomenological/Pragmatic.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: Universality
*   **Evidence:** "Rain wets... Fire burns."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Selection Bias**. While physics is universal, the *implication* is not. As Geertz notes, "Hatlessness is next to godliness" is a valid counter-view to "Come in out of the rain." The Plain Man cherry-picks biological facts to mask cultural values.
    *   *Verdict:* **Shadow**.

#### Criterion B: Handling Anomaly (Intersexuality)
*   **Evidence:** "Horror... Moral enigma."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Catastrophic Failure**. When faced with the Navaho view (Intersexuals as blessed) or Pokot view (Intersexuals as errors), the Plain Man's claim that his reaction is "natural" collapses. His "Common Sense" is revealed to be a specific American cultural prejudice.
    *   *Verdict:* **Void**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE PLAIN MAN
**Score: 45/100 (Fail)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Fatal Flaw:** The "Naturalness" Fallacy. The Plain Man mistakes *familiarity* for *truth*. He cannot explain why other rational people (Zande, Navaho) come to radically different "obvious" conclusions.

# ASSESSMENT 24: CANDIDATE "THE ANTHROPOLOGIST" (Geertz)
**Theoretical Submission:** Common Sense as a Cultural System

### 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest
*   **Core Task:** The candidate redefines Common Sense as an ordered cultural system (like Art or Religion) with specific stylistic features: Naturalness, Practicalness, Thinness, Immethodicalness, and Accessibleness.
*   **Darkness Prior:** Moderate risk of **Over-Interpretation**. Does categorizing "anti-expert" wisdom as a "system" give it too much credit?
*   **Intended Frame:** Interpretive/Symbolic Anthropology.

### 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

#### Criterion A: The Stylistic Features (The "Ness" List)
*   **Evidence:** Defines Common Sense via "Naturalness" (It figures), "Practicalness" (Sagacity), "Thinness" (Literalness), etc.
*   **Guardian Critique:** **High Analytical Utility**. This taxonomy explains *how* Common Sense works rhetorically. It explains why Zande witchcraft feels "natural" to the Zande—it fits the style of their reality.
    *   *Strength:* It relocates authority from the "World" to the "Culture."
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion B: The "Botched Pot" vs. "Gifted Prodigy" (Comparative Analysis)
*   **Evidence:** Contrasting American, Navaho, and Pokot reactions to hermaphroditism.
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Strong Empirical Grounding**. This comparative method effectively dismantles the "Natural" argument. It proves that biology (the anomaly) is constant, but the "Common Sense" reaction is variable.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

#### Criterion C: Immethodicalness (The Ad-Hoc Nature)
*   **Evidence:** "Look before you leap" vs. "He who hesitates is lost."
*   **Guardian Critique:** **Deep Insight**. Geertz correctly identifies that Common Sense does not need to be consistent; it only needs to be *available*. This distinguishes it from Science or Law.
    *   *Verdict:* **Light**.

### 3. Overall Grade: THE ANTHROPOLOGIST
**Score: 95/100 (Distinction)**

**Guardian Justification:**
*   **Paradigm Shift:** Geertz successfully moves Common Sense from the category of "Given" to the category of "Constructed."
*   **Sophistication:** The recognition that Common Sense is a "totalizing" system that rivals Science and Religion is a profound observation. It explains the resistance of the "Plain Man" to expert knowledge.
*   **Final Verdict:** Common Sense is not the absence of artifice; it is the artifice of simplicity.

The Plain Man thinks he is looking through a clear window; The Anthropologist reveals it is a stained-glass window painted by history. By analyzing Common Sense as a cultural system, Geertz gives us the tools to understand why "sanity" sounds different in Zandeland than in Oxford. **Wisdom comes out of the ant-heap, but the ants built the heap.**
