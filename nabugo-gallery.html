Nabugo Gallery

Every builder on this branch, oldest generation first. Each one runs in the browser against the vendored LDraw library — no key, no server.
Everything compiles against ldraw/ in this repo: 16,910 indexed parts with real bounding boxes, 44,329 extracted stud ports. Builds are checked for collisions, support and cohesion before anything is judged, and then measured against 17 real LEGO kits in kits/. Nothing here calls a model.
