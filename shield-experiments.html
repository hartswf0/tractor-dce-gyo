I · Experimental Trajectory

Four Experiments in Operative LEGO Ekphrasis

How we moved from unchecked single-file LDraw dumps to modular, machine-verified LEGO architecture across circular and rectangular stage formats.

Experiment I · Post-Mortem & Craft Rules (docs/sets/README.md)

Why Giant One-Pass LDraw Sets Fail

Auditing the October 2026 Shield renders exposed six structural failure modes that occur whenever a model writes raw .ldr lines in one pass without a coordinate library or checker:

  • Maze Walls & Deep Wells: Tall navy and white walls cut the shield into narrow pens, hiding scenes at the bottom of dark wells. Fix: Frame dividers are 1 plate + 1 tile high (16 LDU), and bands step up toward the boss.
  • Displaced Cosmology: The sky was placed as an oval at the top edge. Fix: Homer places the heavens at the center on the boss (M0).
  • 1×1 Voxel Checkerboards: Random 1×1 bricks stood in for masonry, and heaps of 1×1 round plates stood in for gold. Fix: Coursed Tan/Dark Tan ashlar with LBG corner quoins and continuous 54200 roof slopes.
  • Static Crowds & Equal-Sized Gods: Twenty ungrounded figures stood in a block. Fix: 5–14 acting figures on studs (kitlib.figure) and 1.6× brick-built Golden Ares & Athena.

Experiment III · Ten Lift-Out Radial Kits (shield_frame.py + shield_m0..m9.py · 7,522 pcs)

The 96×96 Circular Relief Shield & Shared Frame Contract

To realize Homer’s five concentric folds (πέντε πτύχες) as a physical kit where ten builders each build one lift-out section (M0–M9), we created a single source of truth in shield_frame.py:

  • Stepped Relief Dome: Four 48×48 baseplates (96×96 studs) step down from +64 LDU on the Boss (M0) to +48 LDU (Cities M1–M2), +32 LDU (Fields M3–M5), and +16 LDU (Pastures/Choros M6–M8 & Oceanus M9).
  • Cross-Bonded Lift-Out Bases: Every section starts with shield_frame.section_base(kit, sec) — two layers of plates laid crosswise (X then Z) covering strictly CELLS[sec], clicking onto SEATS[sec] without touching any neighbor’s cells.
  • Independent & Combined Proof: Every module passes clicks.py with one_piece=True alone and on the 7,522-piece master shield.
Experiment IV · Collector’s Diorama Scaling (shield_diorama_lib.py + shield_dioramas.py · 17,109 pcs)

Scaling All Eight Scenes to Monumental 48×32 Stage Sets

Radial wedges on a circular shield limit deep architectural staging. By foraging five collector’s diorama reference images and scaling every scene to a full 48×32-stud stepped Dark Blue + Pearl Gold museum plinth, we unlocked true architectural depth:

  • Three-Wing Stage Composition: Every 48×32 diorama stages a full rear architectural backdrop (e.g., three 2-storey townhouses in Wedding, a 28-stud Doric Stoa + Archives Tower in Agora, a 34-stud Knossian Palace in Choros).
  • Continuous 54200 Barrel-Tile Roofs: Replaced gapped tile-and-slope mixes with solid ribbed rows of 54200 slopes in Dark Orange (484), Medium Nougat (84), and Reddish Brown (70) over masonry gable tympanums.
  • 17,109 Verified Pieces: All 8 dioramas pass clicks.py with 0 not clicked and 0 in the same space.
II · Procedural LDraw Architecture

How the Kits Were Built: Six Structural Primitives

No LDraw lines were typed by hand. Every set is emitted by Python builders in tools/forage/product/kits/shield_diorama_lib.py and shield_frame.py on top of kitlib.py, then audited by clicks.py.

1. Stepped Kyanos & Gold Museum Plinths

Each 48×32 diorama begins with build_museum_plinth(kit, 48, 32): three cross-bonded plate layers (24 LDU) in Dark Blue (272) kyanos, trimmed with a continuous Pearl Gold (297) tile molding (2431, 3069b) and a centered 8×2 black-and-gold museum plaque on the front riser.

# Layer 1 (X-wise) + Layer 2 (Z-wise)
kit.plate_box(x0, z0, x1, z1, 0,
              P['kyanos'], cross=False)
kit.plate_box(x0, z0, x1, z1, 8,
              P['kyanos'], cross=True)
# Pearl Gold (297) cornice molding
kit.tile_run(x0+1, z1, x1-1, 1,
             16, P['gold'])

2. Continuous 54200 Terracotta Roofs

Mixing 98138 round tiles (8 LDU) with 54200 cheese slopes (16 LDU) along roof eaves creates 1-plate holes. pitched_terracotta_roof() solves this by building stepped Tan/Dark Tan gable tympanum walls and laying continuous ribbed courses of 54200 slopes in Dark Orange (484), Medium Nougat (84), and Reddish Brown (70).

# Continuous ribbed 54200 barrel tiles
for step in range(half):
    ry = y_base + step * 8
    for ix in range(x0, x1 + 1):
        c = ROOF_COLS[(ix*3 + step) % 4]
        kit.put('54200', ix, ry, z_south,
                SOUTH, c)

3. Coursed Ashlar, LBG Quoins & Ivy

build_coursed_box_walls() constructs Tan (19) and Dark Tan (28) limestone walls with interlocking Light Bluish Gray (71) corner quoins, stone/timber doorway lintels, recessed Dark Blue (272) shadow interiors, doorway threshold studs for matrons, and climbing Bright Green (10) ivy.

# Alternating LBG corner quoins + ashlar
is_quoin = (ix <= x0 + 1 or ix >= x1 - 1)
col = P['tin'] if (is_quoin and c_idx % 2 == 1) \
      elseP['stone']
# Recessed Dark Blue shadow interior
kit.brick_box(d_x0, z1-2, d_x1, z1-1,
              y0, d_y1 - y0, P['kyanos'])

4. Doric Stoas, Sacred Circles & Loggias

Civic and palace backdrops use parametric architectural builders: build_doric_stoa() (fluted 2×2 columns, triglyph-and-metope frieze in Dark Blue 272 & Pearl Gold 297), build_sacred_circle_floor() (macaroni tiles 27925 + 8 curved-back elder thrones), and Knossian red columns with Minoan Horns of Consecration.

# Triglyph (272) & Metope (297) frieze
for ix in range(x0, x1 + 1):

         else P['gold']
    kit.put('3024', ix, ent_y+8, z0+1,
            NORTH, fc)

5. Curbed Tree Planters & Livestock

build_raised_tree_planter() frames multi-trunk olive and oak trees inside 4×4 curbed LBG (71) masonry beds filled with Dark Brown (308) soil. build_colossus_god() builds 1.6× tall Pearl Gold Ares & Athena, alongside brick-built gold/tin oxen, tawny maned lions, shepherd hounds, and white sheep.

build_gnarled_tree(kit, cx, cz, y+16)

Every script calls kit.check(one_piece=True), which parses actual ./ldraw/parts/*.dat bounding boxes via ldbox.py to verify that all grid parts belong to one connected stud component with zero volumetric overlaps, before rendering 1440×1080 ACES Filmic studio shots.
