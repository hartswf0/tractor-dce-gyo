# Archive Reframing Analysis

The current `index.html` frames this archive as:
> "Thick Prompts: A Cybernetic Approach to AI Viscosity"

This framing is **hallucinated**. It doesn't match the actual content of the 42 chapters.

## What The Archive Actually Is

### Structure (from reading the .md files)

Each chapter follows a consistent format:

1. **Title**: `When THE [FIGURE A] meets THE [FIGURE B]`
2. **Subtitle**: A thematic title (e.g., "The Architecture of Meaning")
3. **Attribution**: A real thinker (Wittgenstein, Geertz, Winograd, Sontag, etc.)
4. **Dramatic Dialogue**: Two archetypal figures debate
5. **Assessment**: Formal evaluation with scores, criteria, and a winner

### The Thinkers

The chapters draw from:
- **Philosophy of Language**: Wittgenstein, Austin, Searle
- **Anthropology**: Geertz, Ingold
- **AI/Computation**: Winograd, Minsky, Brooks
- **Media Theory**: McLuhan, Sontag, Benjamin
- **Systems Theory**: Bateson, Serres

### The Assessment System

Each chapter ends with:
- **Theoretical Submission**: What each figure argues
- **Calibration & Rubric Digest**: Evaluation criteria
- **Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan**: Detailed critique
- **Score**: Out of 100 (Fail/Pass/Distinction)
- **Winner**: Which position prevails

## What This Archive Is Really About

### Core Theme: **The Epistemology of Intelligent Systems**

The archive stages debates about:
1. **How meaning works** (reference vs. use vs. procedure)
2. **How knowledge is represented** (symbols vs. procedures vs. embodiment)
3. **How intelligence emerges** (planning vs. exploration vs. symbiosis)
4. **How culture shapes cognition** (universal vs. particular)
5. **How media shapes thought** (hot vs. cool, image vs. narrative)

### The "Guardian" Frame

The assessments are written from the perspective of a "Guardian" - an evaluator who:
- Applies a "Darkness Prior" (skepticism toward hidden assumptions)
- Performs "Dark Matter Scans" (looking for unexamined premises)
- Issues verdicts: **Light** (sound), **Shadow** (flawed), **Twilight** (mixed)

This is a **pedagogical device** for teaching critical evaluation of theories.
