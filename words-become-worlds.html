WHAT ISSUE(S) / PROBLEM(S) ARE WE ADDRESSING?

Can we prompt scenarios and explore alternative possibilities?

Most AI tools treat prompts as one-off tricks. We want scenario grounds where you can rehearse hard choices, branch paths, and come back to the same world later.

We are exploring the spatial possibilities of AI: instead of single answers, we care about grids, rooms, timelines, and routes players can walk.

We also want a space between LEGO and Roblox: for people who love building worlds but are scared of being trapped in a platform.

WHAT I CREATED · WAG / LEGOS STUDIO FAMILY

WAG (Words Assemble Geometry / Grids / Generations) claims words don’t just describe worlds, they compile them. LEGOS chat logs, scripts, and briefings become 3D models and social grids that democratize design.

Under the hood, this is a small GAME ENGINE made of:

  • General-purpose utilities for mapping stories into channels, scenes, and 9×9 grids.
  • Two GPT orbits (scenario generator + structure weaver).
  • LEGOS surfaces where words become worlds you can replay.
  • Vignettes and studios for planning, documentation, and film experiments.

Thesis. WAG (Words Assemble Geometry / Grids / Generations) claims words don’t describe worlds, they compile them. Natural language turns into 3D models and social grids that democratize design and, via Freirean pedagogy, make tech a tool of liberation.

Imagine if you could build anything just by talking about it’like telling a story, but the computer actually makes what you're saying appear in front of you. You don’t need fancy degrees or complicated software. You just say ‘make a red box’ or ‘build me a little house’ and it happens. WAG is a "Wild Ass Guess" approach: try things, see what works, and learn as you go.

The point is giving regular people’not just tech experts’t he power to bring their ideas to life, and to keep listening to collaborators about how to make it easier and more just.
