1 · What HOMER is Good For

HOMER is the place where the labs line up. Instead of opening separate tabs for Swiss, Frank, Courage, Weaver, Were, Master, and Mento, you dock them as columns and walk a single scene from MPD text, to GOLD skeletons, to capture.

SWISS · choose a scene
FRANK · track scenes
COURAGE · edit & view MPD
WEAVER · GOLD / skeleton
WERE · skeleton MPD lab
MASTER · overlay & tune
MENTO · record shots

Think of HOMER as a story bench: Swiss names the scene, Frank tracks it, Courage holds the MPD, Weaver turns MPD + GOLD into a map of studs, Were/MASTER let you probe and correct, and Mento records the performance.

Swiss builders (inside Swiss)
  • SCENEERATOR – pick a shell, send scene-mpd to Frank / Courage.
  • LOCATIONATOR – dress the shell, send location-mpd on the bus.
  • VEHICULATOR – build vehicles, export vehicle MPDs for Frank's grid cells.
  • MINIFIGURATOR – build characters / families, send minifig MPDs into Frank.

All of these -ators speak MPD and ship their work over wag-frank. Frank catches them, composes a plate, and HOMER can then emit that combined scene into Courage.

Deep dive · Swiss → Frank → Courage
1 · In Courage · Run RB

In Courage's viewer toolbar, on the far right beside IMG, GOLD and GLB, there is an RB button (Red Bull). Click it to:

  • Walk the current MPD line by line.
  • Generate a stud_skeleton_v2 entry for every stud.
  • Store the GOLD scene in localStorage.wag_redbull_gold.
  • Broadcast a redbull-gold message on the wag-frank bus.
Deep dive · Courage RB → GOLD
2 · In Weaver · Assembly Line → XRAY RB → GOLD bus

Switch to Weaver in HOMER. With GOLD now available:

  • Hit Assembly Line to expand the GOLD scene into per-part stud skeletons and StudMap MPD.
  • Hit XRAY RB to inspect the sampled studs and see coverage / gaps.
  • When satisfied, hit GOLD bus to push this processed GOLD scene onto the shared bus.

That GOLD payload is what Were and Master listen for.

Deep dive · Weaver Assembly Line / XRAY / GOLD bus
3 · In Master · Tune, then Save + Copy

In Master, open the same scene. Master overlays GOLD studs on a compact MPD editor so you can:

  • Move bricks and cells more thoughtfully on the grid.
  • Adjust skeleton flips and offsets per part / per line.

Once the overlay looks correct, use Save + Copy (or the export / copy controls) in Master. HOMER intercepts this as a master-export-mpd event and routes the tuned MPD straight back into Courage (and Weaver), so the editor and skeleton stay in sync.

Deep dive · Master tuning → Courage
4 · Back in Courage · Export / Capture

Return to Courage. You're now looking at the refined scene with:

  • MPD text that includes all the Master tweaks.
  • A stud skeleton that reflects the GOLD bus updates.

From here you can:

  • Export stills with IMG.
  • Export geometry with GLB.
  • Switch to Mento and record motion via the shared Courage viewer.
5 · In Mento · Record Brickfilm Shots

Open Mento in HOMER. When you click Mento in SINGLE mode, HOMER docks Courage + Mento together:

  • Set aspect to 4:3 and choose a quality (480p / 720p / 960p).
  • Select a path (manual orbit, orbit ring, push-through-center).
  • Hit REC – the header flips to REC, a subtle sound / vibrate confirms capture started.
  • Hit SAVE – downloads a WebM named mento_<scene>_<timestamp>.webm and adds it to the mini gallery.

Courage remains the source of truth for MPD, screenshots (IMG) and GLB export; Mento is your camera instrument pointed at that stage.

Troubleshooting · When HOMER misbehaves
  • If the shell feels stuck (columns blank, panels not responding), you can refresh the browser tab. Treat this as a hard reset: layout and any unsaved text edits in Courage / Master may be lost, so export MPD or use Save + Copy first.
  • If Courage looks empty, re-emit the scene: click a Swiss scene brick or run a builder's Save MPD again, or use Frank's scene emit control so wag-frank-scene.mpd is sent back into HOMER.
  • You can keep adding material: build extra minifig families, vehicles, or location chunks in Swiss and send them again – Frank adds them to its grid, and the next emit updates Courage's stage.
4 · Mental Model · How to Navigate HOMER
Tabs
  • Swiss – choose which scene / catalog you are working on.
  • Frank – see a grid of scenes and families over time.
  • Courage – treat MPD as source code for the scene.
  • Weaver – turn GOLD into per-line, per-part maps.
  • Were – experiment with a skeleton-only MPD form.
  • Master – overlay GOLD on a compact editor, then commit.
  • Mento – record the result as video clips.
Modes
  • SINGLE – one panel at a time, full width. Good for phones.
  • MULTI – dock several panels as columns. Good for lab work.
  • Clicking Mento in SINGLE mode automatically opens Courage + Mento together so you always see the same scene you are recording.

When in doubt, start in Swiss to pick a scene, move to Courage to edit, use Red Bull to emit GOLD, weave and correct in Weaver / Master, then finish in Mento with a recorded shot.
