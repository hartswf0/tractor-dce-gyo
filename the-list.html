Ned's record

five red apples
green bananas
plain flour ?
A description. Ned Flanders follows you and writes down what goes in the cart. If his record and the cart disagree, his record is wrong, and he fixes it. When a shelf hides what you take, he guesses.
THE CART➞THE RECORD word-to-world

This is Elizabeth Anscombe's example in Intention (1957, §32): a man shopping with a list, and a detective behind him writing down what he buys. The two lists can carry the same words and still work in opposite directions. If the list and the basket don't agree, the mistake is in what the man did. If the record and the basket don't agree, the mistake is in the record. Later philosophers called this direction of fit.

Five red apples is Wittgenstein's slip (Philosophical Investigations, §1). The shopkeeper finds the drawer marked APPLES, looks up RED against a colour sample, and counts to five as he takes them out. The words mean nothing without those three routines. In the store you do all three: the crate is marked APPLES, a red sample card sits beside it, and you take the apples one at a time.

The store

In frontThe vestibule with the carts, the five checkout lanes, the service desk, the bakery (French bread, buns, croissants, a cake, pies, cupcakes, doughnuts) and the flowers.
Left wallProduce: the misted rack, the banana stand (green bananas on one half, yellow on the other), the fruit crates (the red and green APPLES crates first), the berry case.
Aisles 1 to 7Numbered boards over each aisle. Aisle 3 is oils: the extra virgin sits next to the light, and the tag tells them apart. Aisle 4 is baking: the organic flour sits next to the plain. End caps carry Buzz Cola, Duff, Krusty-O's, Lard Lad and Mr. Sparkle.
Right wallFrozen, behind glass doors: the southwest style hash browns next to the regular.
BackDairy along the wall, the meat and seafood counter in the corner, the ice cream chest.

The store is a real LEGO model of 11,122 pieces, every one supported (world/models/supermarket.js): the manual is in play/models/supermarket.json, and it opens in Hand Butter as thirteen zones (Hand Butter).
