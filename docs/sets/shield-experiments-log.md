# The Shield of Achilles — Experimental Architecture Log & How the Kits Were Built

This document records the complete sequence of engineering and design experiments that produced the **Shield of Achilles** LEGO/LDraw line (*Iliad* XVIII.478–608): from diagnosing the October 2026 failure modes, through the **96×96 Five-Ring Circular Relief Shield** (`7,522` pieces across 10 independent lift-out sections `M0–M9`), to the **Eight Monumental `48×32` Collector's Diorama Stage Sets** (`17,109` pieces on stepped `Dark Blue 272` + `Pearl Gold 297` museum plinths).

Every build across both systems is generated by deterministic Python programs on `tools/forage/product/kitlib.py` and verified by `tools/forage/product/clicks.py` with **0 parts not clicked** and **0 pairs of parts in the same space**.

## 1. Experiment I — Post-Mortem of the October 2026 Failure Modes

Before writing a single new builder, we audited why earlier attempts to generate large LEGO sets from Homer failed (`docs/sets/README.md`):

| Failure Mode | What Went Wrong in October 2026 | The Craft Rule That Replaced It |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Maze Walls & Deep Wells** | Tall navy and white walls cut the shield into narrow pens; scenes sat at the bottom of dark wells invisible from hero camera angles. | **Dividers belong to the frame (`shield_frame.py`)** and are only **one plate and one tile high (16 LDU)**. Scenes step *up* toward the central boss or rise against rear architectural backdrops. |
| **2. Displaced Cosmology** | The heavens were placed as an oval at the top edge of the board. | **Homer puts the heavens at the centre, on the boss (`M0`)**: Earth, Sea, Sky, Sun, Moon, and the revolving Bear (`61485c01` turntable) crown the highest terrace (`+64 LDU`). |
| **3. Voxel Checkerboards** | 1×1 bricks and plates in random colours stood in for wall texture; heaps of 1×1 round plates stood in for gold and crowds. | **Texture from structure**: coursed `Tan (19)` and `Dark Tan (28)` ashlar masonry with `Light Bluish Gray (71)` corner quoins, stone lintels, and continuous `54200` terracotta barrel-tile roofs. |
| **4. Static Blocks of Figures** | Twenty figures stood in a block doing nothing; gods were the same size as mortals; figures floated or overlapped. | **Figures act on studs (`kitlib.figure`)**: 1+ stud spacing, posed toward a single dramatic focal point, with **Ares and Pallas Athena built at 1.6× divine scale in Pearl Gold (297)**. |
| **5. Staircase Circles** | Concentric rings were built from square bricks, reading as jagged pixel stairs. | **Curved ring edges**: quarter-circle macaroni tiles (`27925`, `27507`), round corner tiles (`25269`, `79393`), and round corner plates (`30357`, `30565`) finish every arc. |
| **6. Unchecked Single-File Dumps** | A 3,000-line `.mpd` was emitted in one pass without coordinate measurement or visual inspection. | **The Builder/Reviewer Loop**: every script runs `kitlib.check` (`clicks.py` with `one_piece=True`) and renders multi-angle studio sheets via `shoot.py` (`--az 185 --el 26`, `--el 5`, `--el 88`). |

## 2. Experiment II — The 96×96 Circular Shield & Ten Lift-Out Kits (`M0–M9`)

### Shared Coordinate & Interface Contract (`tools/forage/product/kits/shield_frame.py`)
To build a `96×96`-stud circular shield (`76.8 × 76.8 cm` on four `4186` `48×48` black baseplates) where ten builders can work independently without a single clashing brick, we separated **the Frame** from **the Sections**:

- **Stepped Concentric Relief Dome (`BAND_Y`)**:
  Instead of flat pens behind tall walls, the frame steps *down* outward from the central boss so every ring is visible from a 24° hero angle:
  - **Ring 1 — Boss (`M0`, $r \le 10$ studs)**: Seat at `64 LDU` (8 plates above baseplate). Section top at `80 LDU`.
  - **Ring 2 — Two Cities (`M1` Peace, `M2` War, $11 \le r \le 21$)**: Seat at `48 LDU`. Section top at `64 LDU`.
  - **Ring 3 — Three Fields (`M3` Plough, `M4` Harvest, `M5` Vineyard, $22 \le r \le 32$)**: Seat at `32 LDU`. Section top at `48 LDU`.
  - **Ring 4 — Pastures & Choros (`M6` Cattle/Lions, `M7` Sheepfold, `M8` Choros, $33 \le r \le 41$)**: Seat at `16 LDU`. Section top at `32 LDU`.
  - **Ring 5 — River Oceanus (`M9`, $42 \le r \le 45$)**: Seat at `16 LDU`, enclosed by the triple glittering rim (`r = 45..47`).
- **Two-Layer Cross-Bonded Lift-Out Base (`shield_frame.section_base`)**:
  Each section `M0–M9` is built on two layers of plates laid crosswise (layer 1 horizontal along X, layer 2 vertical along Z), covering *only* `CELLS[sec]`. Because the two plate layers are orthogonally bonded and clicked onto `SEATS[sec]`, each section passes `clicks.py` with `one_piece=True` on its own and drops cleanly into `set.shield-of-achilles.mpd`.

## 3. Experiment III — Foraging the Collector's Diorama Aesthetic (`shield_diorama_lib.py`)

While the circular shield solves the concentric cosmology of Hephaestus's object, each sector of a circular ring is geometrically constrained to a radial wedge. To achieve the architectural depth, theatrical staging, and structural richness of flagship LEGO Ideas / Icons historical sets, we studied five reference collector's dioramas (`ref_wedding.jpg`, `ref_agora.jpg`, `ref_ambush.jpg`, `ref_sheepfold.jpg`, `ref_harvest.jpg`) and extracted a reusable **Collector's Diorama Grammar** in `tools/forage/product/kits/shield_diorama_lib.py`:

1. **Stepped `Dark Blue (272)` + `Pearl Gold (297)` Museum Plinth (`build_museum_plinth`)**:
   - Every diorama stands on a 3-plate (`24 LDU`) cross-bonded `Dark Blue (272)` kyanos plinth with a stepped 1-stud outer ledge, a continuous `Pearl Gold (297)` tile molding (`2431`, `3069b`, `3070b`), and a centered `8×2` black-and-gold museum nameplate on the front riser.
2. **Continuous Ribbed Terracotta Barrel-Tile Roofs (`pitched_terracotta_roof`)**:
   - Early experiments mixed `98138` 1×1 round tiles (8 LDU high) with `54200` 1×1×2/3 cheese slopes (16 LDU high) on roof eaves, leaving visible 1-plate holes between rows.
   - We solved this by building stepped `Tan`/`Dark Tan` masonry gable tympanums under every roof course and laying **continuous ribbed rows of `54200` cheese slopes** alternating `Dark Orange (484)`, `Medium Nougat (84)`, and `Reddish Brown (70)` along X, crowned with a raised `Reddish Brown` ridge spine capped by `297 Pearl Gold` and `484 Dark Orange` `98138` round tiles.
3. **Coursed Ashlar Masonry with `LBG (71)` Corner Quoins & Recessed Interiors (`build_coursed_box_walls`)**:
   - Walls are never flat monochrome slabs: they alternate `Tan (19)` and `Dark Tan (28)` courses with `Light Bluish Gray (71)` interlocking corner quoins at every vertical edge, `LBG`/`Reddish Brown` lintels over doorways, recessed `Dark Blue (272)` shadow interiors, timber window shutters, and `Bright Green (10)` climbing ivy (`build_climbing_vine`) with `98138` blossom studs.
4. **Raised Stone Tree Planters & Gnarled Mediterranean Flora (`build_raised_tree_planter`, `build_gnarled_tree`, `build_cypress_tree`)**:
   - Street and courtyard trees stand inside `4×4` curbed `LBG (71)` stone planters filled with `Dark Brown (308)` soil, with multi-trunk `Reddish Brown` bark and layered `Olive Green (326)`, `Dark Green (288)`, and `Bright Green (10)` foliage canopies studded with `Pearl Gold (297)` olives.
5. **Brick-Built Mythic Scale & Livestock (`build_colossus_god`, `build_ox`, `build_lion`, `build_sheep`, `build_dog`)**:
   - **Ares & Pallas Athena (`build_colossus_god`)**: 1.6× tall brick-built `Pearl Gold (297)` colossi with `Metallic Silver (80)` / `Metallic Copper (300)` greaves, hoplon shields (`75902`), spears (`4499`), and high-crested helmets.
   - **Livestock**: Articulated brick-built straight-horned oxen in `Pearl Gold (297)` and `Light Bluish Gray (71)` tin, tawny maned lions (`19 Tan` / `484 Dark Orange` / `28 Dark Tan`), `15 White` sheep with `LBG` fleeces, and `15 White` shepherd hounds.
