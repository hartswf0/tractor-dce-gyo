From Desert Lab to Courage Studio
The MENTO series is a chain of camera tools that all share the same core idea:

Use a calm path editor (MENTO) to drive a rich 3D stage (GLB or Courage), then capture deterministic camera moves at film-friendly aspect ratios.

This guide walks from the original mento-glb-desert.html through mento-368/369 and into the new mento-372/373 surfaces that ride directly on Courage via Homer shells like Shield of Achilles and the compact Ball Jar.

Use 368 as Your Baseline Instrument
MENTO-368 is the clean, GLB-first camera surface:

• Works entirely on its own canvas.
• Owns the scene, camera, and path.
• Decouples preview time (real-time) from capture time (virtual seconds).

Use 368 when you just want to load a GLB, carve a path, and export WebM – no HOMER shell or Courage integration required.

const win = frame.contentWindow;
const iface = win.MomentoInterface || win.VideoCaptureInterface;
const captureData = win._captureData || {};
const scene = iface?.scene || captureData.scene;
const camera = iface?.camera || captureData.camera;

Prototype: Bring MENTO to the Stage
MENTO-372 is a lab surface, not the final instrument:

• Courage still renders its own scene and GOLD.
• MENTO mounts as a child iframe and pokes at Courage via MomentoInterface.
• It proved that we can treat Courage as the “camera body” and MENTO as the lens / path brain.

372 is documented so we remember the experiment, but 373 is the surface you'll actually use for brickfilm work.

Use 373 as the Camera Head on the HOMER Chain
In Shield of Achilles:

• The center panel is still Courage – the only place that actually renders the stage and emits GOLD.
• The right panel is now MENTO-373, a nearly 1:1 clone of 371 re-targeted to Courage's THREE.Scene and camera.
• GOLD snapshots from Courage and Weaver flow into MENTO-373, which parses !MENTO SHOT / !MENTO LIGHT, builds keyframes, mirrors lights + splines into Courage, and runs deterministic capture over the Courage framebuffer.

This is the “full studio” surface when you want Swiss/Frank, Weaver, WERE, Master, and MENTO all working on the same MPD.

BALL JAR & GRECIAN URN
6
⬇️ TWO JARS
BALL JAR
Courage · MENTO-373

ball-jar.html is a tiny shell with only two panels: Courage on the left, MENTO-373 on the right. It still listens to the wag-frank bus for GOLD, but drops all the extra labs so you can focus on camera paths and capture tuning.

GRECIAN URN
Swiss · Frank · Courage · Weaver · Were · Master · Mento-373

grecian-urn.html is a full HOMER-style shell with the whole Swiss → Frank → Courage → Weaver → Were → Master chain, but it boots like Ball Jar: Courage and MENTO-373 are paired and docked by default, with the other labs one tab away.

Pick the Right Jar for the Job
Ball Jar is the jar on the desk version of Shield:

• Courage renders the stage exactly as in HOMER.
• 373 draws its spline, key markers, and frustum helpers over a preview of Courage's framebuffer.
• Capture records from a dedicated capture canvas sized by ASPECT × RES, using Courage's WebGL canvas as the source.

Grecian Urn keeps that Ball-Jar pairing but restores the full chain around it:

• Same Courage+373 default layout as Ball Jar.
• Swiss, Frank, Weaver, WERE, and Master are still present as dockable panels.
• GOLD and MPD loops mirror Shield of Achilles, but with a Courage/MENTO-centric chrome.

Use Ball Jar when you only care about camera + capture. Use Grecian Urn when you want that same view inside a complete HOMER studio.
